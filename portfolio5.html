Kosmok - WIDMO

I've had a great opportunity to cooperate with the amazing professional team at Kosmok and especially its founder Łucja Rugor on the project WIDMO. WIDMO is a photorealistic simulation for dataset generation for satellite pose estimation, built in Blender at an almost planetary scale, using the Tango spacecraft from the PRISMA mission as a base for training ML models. Taking part in its creation gave me the opportunity to develop many existing skills and discover new ones. This project demanded a huge amount of cooperation, precision, research and problem-solving — and all of it led to a great outcome.
